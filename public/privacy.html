Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026
Dashboard

1. Geolocation Data

This weather application requests access to your browser GPS coordinates solely to provide local meteorological conditions, hourly timelines, and severe storm warnings. Your geographic coordinates are held only in client memory during your active session. Coordinates are never saved to external tracking databases, shared with advertisers, or transmitted to analytics services.

2. Client Local Storage

Your unit preference (Celsius or Fahrenheit) and recent city search history are stored locally on your device via browser localStorage. This data remains on your hardware and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser cache or site data.

3. Meteorological Service Provider

Atmospheric forecasts and geocoding city queries are processed through Open-Meteo APIs without requiring private user API keys. For information regarding their technical infrastructure and privacy practices, consult the Open-Meteo Terms and Privacy Documentation.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions or feedback regarding this weather dashboard, visit the project repository documentation or reach out via official project channels.
